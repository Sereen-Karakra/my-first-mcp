## Role Responsibilities

### 1. Algorithm Lead — Maysam Abu Eid

- Understand and use the provided mouse HAL interface (`mouse_hal.h`).
- Develop the maze-solving strategy and the solver (`solver.c`).
- Track the mouse position, heading, and discovered maze walls.
- Test and debug the solver in the mms simulator.

### 2. Mechanical Lead — Dania Abuayyash

- Design the chassis and manage the mechanical assembly.
- Check dimensions, component mounting, and mechanical fit.
- Coordinate 3D printing and mechanical testing.
- Maintain the mass and size budget; document the mechanical design and changes.

### 3. Electronics Lead — Raneem Alkhawaja

- Prepare the schematic, electronics plan and pin table.
- Handle sensor and motor-driver wiring with the team.
- Check power requirements and safe connections.
- Maintain the bill of materials; document the circuit and electronics tests.

### 4. Motion Lead — Sereen Karakra

- Work on motor control and PWM.
- Calibrate encoders and distance measurement.
- Develop and test straight movement and turns.
- Tune movement control (PID).

### 5. Integration & Testing Lead and Algorithm Co-lead — Masa Awad

- Coordinate integration between the mechanical, electronics, motion, and algorithm subsystems.
- Maintain system-level test cases and record results.
- Track integration issues and coordinate debugging with subsystem owners.
- Collect milestone test evidence and help verify acceptance criteria.
- Co-develop and test the solver (test mazes, map dump) with the Algorithm Lead.

## Team Working Agreement

- Each member is responsible for understanding and explaining her assigned work.
- Each member contributes and commits her own work to the repository, at least twice a week, with clear commit messages.
- Tasks are assigned to individual members on the team Trello board (one owner per card).
- Every member posts a weekly update on the "Weekly log" card: what I did, what failed, what I do next.
- Members support one another during integration and testing.
- Work on the solver is shared so that every member can explain it.
- All members participate in milestone demos and can explain the overall robot.
- Any AI assistance is used only as a tutor (explaining concepts or errors) and is logged on the relevant Trello card.
